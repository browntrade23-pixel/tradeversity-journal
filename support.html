Account
I didn't get the verification email

Check your Spam and Promotions folders. The code expires after 1 hour: on the verification screen, tap "Send a new code". Make sure the email address is written correctly, or tap "Change email".

I forgot my password

On the login screen, type your email first, then tap "Forgot password?". You will receive an email to choose a new password.

My account is waiting for approval

After you verify your email and your Telegram, the team approves new members. You can use the app as soon as your account is approved.

Telegram
How do I verify my Telegram?

Open our bot @Tardeversity_verify_bot on Telegram and press Start. It sends you a 6-digit code: type it in the app. The code works for 15 minutes, with 5 tries. Your Telegram username in your profile must be the one you use on Telegram.

Mentorship course
Where do I watch the course?

On a Windows PC, in the Cerebra by TDV app. On Android, in the Cerebra by TDV app for Android. iPhone and iPad are not supported yet. Open the Mentorship course in your account: it shows the right download for your device.

I have a new phone or PC

Move the course yourself: on the new device, ask for a code sent to your Telegram and type it. The new device replaces the old one 24 hours later, and your old device can cancel the change. You can move it once every 90 days, 2 times a year.

Why does the course open on one device only?

To protect the lessons from sharing and leaks. Your name and email also appear on every video, so a leak shows who it came from.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Windows shows this because the app is new and not yet signed with a paid certificate, not because it is harmful. Only download it from jointradeversity.com.

Android or Play Protect warns me

If Chrome asks, tap "Download anyway". If Play Protect warns, tap "More details", then "Install anyway". It warns about any app that doesn't come from Google Play.

Trading journal
How do I import my trades?

In My trading, use "Import CSV". It reads exports from Tradovate, NinjaTrader and MT5, and detects your prop firm, platform and commissions.

Who can see my journal?

Only you. A share link is read-only and you can turn it off at any time. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Is TRADEVERSITY financial advice?

No. Everything is education only. Trading is risky: never risk money you can't afford to lose.
