Privacy policy

TRADEVERSITY website and the Cerebra by TDV apps (Windows and Android) · Last updated: November 27, 2026

In short: we collect only what the trading journal, the tools and the protected Mentorship course need. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, we don't use advertising trackers, and the apps can't read your contacts, files, camera, microphone or location.

Who we are

TRADEVERSITY (jointradeversity.com) is a trading journal, a set of trading tools and a Mentorship course by Brown Algo · ZN Trading. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Cerebra by TDV is our app for Windows and Android that plays the course in a protected window. Questions? Our support team is here to help: contact it from jointradeversity.com/support.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, name and Telegram username, and the profile details you choose to add (for example username, country, city, date of birth, phone number, photo). If you link our Telegram bot, your Telegram chat ID so it can send you the messages you choose. Your password is handled by our login provider and stored only in encrypted (hashed) form: we never see it.
  • Your trading journal: the trades, plans, notes, emotions, screenshots, imported CSV files, reviews and settings you enter.
  • The Mentorship course (members only): your course device record (a security key created on your device and a device name such as "Windows PC · Cerebra by TDV"), a course security log (date and time, action, IP address, country, browser or app type), your lesson progress, your assignments and your mentor's feedback.
  • Help center form: your email, the topic and your message, and the IP address the message was sent from. We use the IP address only to stop spam (a limited number of messages per hour).
  • Security check: when you sign up, log in or reset your password, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person and not a bot. Cloudflare looks at technical signals from your browser for this.
  • Two-step login (admin accounts): the secret for the authenticator app is kept by our login provider.
  • App error reports: when a screen of the website breaks, the technical error is sent to our database so we can fix it: the error message, the file and line, the page (for example "#journal"), the app version, your browser type and, if you are logged in, your account ID. Email addresses and long numbers in the message are masked, and no journal content is sent.
  • Partner links: when you click a prop firm link, which firm and where on the page, to count referrals.
  • Links you share: the share links you create yourself.
  • Your consent: the version and date of the terms and privacy policy you accepted, saved on your account as proof.
  • Discord (optional): if you join our Discord server and open a support ticket, Discord and our own ticket bot handle your Discord username and the messages in that ticket.

Stored on your device

The platform saves some things in your browser so it works the way you left it: your login session, language, theme, background, last opened tab, risk calculator presets, prop firm rules, notification status, your choice about TradingView content and similar settings. They stay on your device and you can remove them by clearing the site data in your browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

On the free public tool pages (risk calculator, consistency rule calculator, prop firm coupons), what you type stays in your browser: it is saved only on your device to remember your settings, and it is never sent to us. Partner links on the coupon page open the prop firm's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

What we don't collect

No contacts, no files from your device (only the files you choose to upload), no camera, no microphone, no GPS location, no advertising ID. No ads and no selling or renting of your data.

The Cerebra by TDV apps

  • The apps open jointradeversity.com in a protected window that screenshots and screen recordings can't capture.
  • On Windows, the app checks on your PC whether a screen-recording program is open, to pause the lesson until it's closed. This check stays on your PC: nothing about your programs is sent to us.
  • Your login and course device key stay on your device inside the app.
  • The versions downloaded from our website check jointradeversity.com for updates. The Microsoft Store and Google Play versions are updated by the store.

Why we use it (and on what basis)

  • To run your account, your journal and the tools you use (needed to provide the service you asked for).
  • To protect the course: one device per member, encrypted videos, and your name and email shown on the videos, so any leak can be traced (needed to provide the course and to protect our rights).
  • To send the Telegram reminders and notifications you turn on (your choice, you can turn them off).
  • To keep the service secure, stop spam and abuse, and improve it (our legitimate interest).

Services we use

  • Supabase: login, database and server functions.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: the security check on sign-up, login and password reset.
  • VdoCipher: encrypted video streaming for the course.
  • Telegram and Discord: the messages you choose to receive, and support tickets.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.
  • TradingView: the live news and calendar widgets on the News page. They load only after you agree, and you can turn them off again in My profile.
  • Forex Factory: the source of the economic calendar. Our server collects it and stores it in our database, so your browser never contacts Forex Factory.
  • Fonts and code libraries are hosted on our own website: loading them sends nothing to Google or other companies.

Each of these services processes data only to provide its part of the service, under its own privacy policy. Some of them process data outside Algeria (for example in the European Union or the United States).

How long we keep it

  • Your account, journal and course data: as long as your account exists. When your account is deleted, they are deleted with it.
  • Help center messages: as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the request.
  • App error reports: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Backups: we keep private weekly backups of the database to restore the service after a failure. They are stored securely by the TRADEVERSITY team, and data you deleted can remain in an older backup for a limited time until that backup is replaced.

Your choices and rights

You can see and edit your profile and journal in the platform at any time, and export your trades as a CSV file. You can also ask to get a copy of your data, correct it, delete your account, or object to a use of your data, as provided by the data protection law that applies to you, including Algerian Law No. 18-07 of June 10, 2018 on the protection of personal data. In My profile → Your data you can download your data (Download my data) and ask for your account to be deleted (Delete my account). For anything else, contact our support team from jointradeversity.com/support. We answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the data protection authority of your country (in Algeria, the ANPDP).

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Each member can only access their own data (access rules in the database), the login has a security check against bots, admin accounts use two-step login, the support form is protected against spam, course videos are encrypted, and the database is backed up every week.

Who can see your data: only the TRADEVERSITY admins, a small team appointed by the founders, to run the service, approve accounts, follow the Mentorship and answer the support team's requests. Every admin is bound to confidentiality and must use two-step login.

Age

TRADEVERSITY is for adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

When we add a feature or change how we use data, we update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we also tell you in the platform. See also our Terms and conditions.

En bref : nous collectons uniquement ce dont le journal de trading, les outils et la formation protégée ont besoin. Nous ne vendons pas vos données, nous n'affichons pas de publicité, nous n'utilisons pas de traceurs publicitaires, et les applications ne peuvent pas lire vos contacts, fichiers, caméra, micro ni votre position.

Qui sommes-nous

TRADEVERSITY (jointradeversity.com) est un journal de trading, un ensemble d'outils de trading et une formation (Mentorship) par Brown Algo · ZN Trading. Nous sommes responsables des données personnelles décrites ici. Cerebra by TDV est notre application Windows et Android qui lit la formation dans une fenêtre protégée. Une question ? Notre équipe support est là pour vous aider : contactez-la depuis jointradeversity.com/support.

Ce que nous collectons

  • Votre compte : adresse e-mail, nom et nom d'utilisateur Telegram, et les informations de profil que vous choisissez d'ajouter (par exemple nom d'utilisateur, pays, ville, date de naissance, téléphone, photo). Si vous reliez notre bot Telegram, votre identifiant de discussion Telegram pour vous envoyer les messages que vous choisissez. Votre mot de passe est géré par notre service de connexion et stocké uniquement sous forme chiffrée (hachée) : nous ne le voyons jamais.
  • Votre journal de trading : les trades, plans, notes, émotions, captures, fichiers CSV importés, bilans et réglages que vous saisissez.
  • La formation (membres uniquement) : l'enregistrement de votre appareil (une clé de sécurité créée sur votre appareil et un nom comme « Windows PC · Cerebra by TDV »), un journal de sécurité (date et heure, action, adresse IP, pays, type de navigateur ou d'application), votre progression, vos devoirs et les retours de votre mentor.
  • Formulaire du centre d'aide : votre e-mail, le sujet et votre message, ainsi que l'adresse IP d'envoi. Nous utilisons l'adresse IP uniquement contre le spam (nombre de messages limité par heure).
  • Vérification de sécurité : à l'inscription, à la connexion et à la réinitialisation du mot de passe, Cloudflare Turnstile vérifie que vous êtes une personne et non un robot. Cloudflare analyse pour cela des signaux techniques de votre navigateur.
  • Connexion en deux étapes (comptes admin) : le secret de l'application d'authentification est conservé par notre service de connexion.
  • Rapports d'erreurs de l'app : quand un écran du site plante, l'erreur technique est envoyée à notre base de données pour que nous la corrigions : le message d'erreur, le fichier et la ligne, la page (par exemple « #journal »), la version de l'app, le type de navigateur et, si vous êtes connecté, l'identifiant de votre compte. Les adresses e-mail et les longs nombres dans le message sont masqués, et aucun contenu de votre journal n'est envoyé.
  • Liens partenaires : quand vous cliquez sur le lien d'une prop firm, laquelle et à quel endroit de la page, pour compter les parrainages.
  • Liens partagés : les liens de partage que vous créez vous-même.
  • Votre consentement : la version et la date des conditions et de la politique de confidentialité que vous avez acceptées, enregistrées sur votre compte comme preuve.
  • Discord (facultatif) : si vous rejoignez notre serveur Discord et ouvrez un ticket, Discord et notre propre bot de tickets traitent votre nom d'utilisateur Discord et les messages de ce ticket.

Enregistré sur votre appareil

La plateforme enregistre certaines choses dans votre navigateur pour fonctionner comme vous l'avez laissée : votre session de connexion, la langue, le thème, l'arrière-plan, le dernier onglet ouvert, les préréglages du calculateur de risque, les règles de prop firm, l'état des notifications, votre choix concernant le contenu TradingView et réglages similaires. Ils restent sur votre appareil et vous pouvez les supprimer en effaçant les données du site dans votre navigateur. Nous n'utilisons pas de cookies publicitaires ou de suivi.

Sur les pages d'outils gratuits ouvertes à tous (calculateur de risque, calculateur de règle de consistance, codes promo des prop firms), ce que vous tapez reste dans votre navigateur : c'est enregistré uniquement sur votre appareil pour garder vos réglages, et ce n'est jamais envoyé chez nous. Les liens partenaires de la page des codes promo ouvrent le site de la prop firm, qui a sa propre politique de confidentialité.

Aucun contact, aucun fichier de votre appareil (seulement ceux que vous choisissez de téléverser), ni caméra, ni micro, ni position GPS, ni identifiant publicitaire. Aucune publicité, aucune vente ni location de vos données.
